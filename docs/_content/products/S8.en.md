### Product overview

Distance sensing can support obstacle detection, proximity feedback and spacing control. Read a value, evaluate a condition and trigger an appropriate action.

### Before you begin

1. Confirm your product version and controller. Follow the official wiring diagram.
2. Install the required programming environment and product driver.
3. Check raw readings before adding conditions or motor control.

::: tip Start with a reading
Begin by displaying a value. Confirm its units and stability before testing threshold logic. Wiring diagrams and driver links will be added when supplied.
:::

### Applications

- **Obstacle detection**: stop or turn when a reading is below a threshold.
- **Proximity feedback**: show a message or sound based on distance.
- **Spacing control**: use continuous readings as an input to your control logic.
