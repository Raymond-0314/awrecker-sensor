The LineSensor8 is a high-precision, 8-channel line-following sensor designed specifically for WRO robotics competitions. Featuring <span style="color: red;">8-channel infrared detection</span> and <span style="color: red;">intelligent interference-rejection algorithms</span>, it effectively reduces false detections caused by colored patterns on complex tracks. It supports 5-second, one-button calibration and provides high-resolution line position, line width, and sensor status data. Combined with PID control, it enables smoother, more stable, and more precise line following.

## Before You Begin

1. Confirm that the MBC S8 Line-Following Sensor version matches your controller.
2. Connect your MBC S8 Line-Following Sensor to the controller using a cable.
3. After power is supplied, the green heartbeat indicator near the connector will begin flashing.

## Calibration and Normalization

1. Press the calibration button.
    - Hold the calibration button on the sensor for 0.5 seconds until the 🟢 green calibration indicator near the button stays on.
2. Begin scanning (5 seconds).
    - Move the sensor back and forth over the black and white areas of the track.
    - Scan the darkest and brightest areas.
    - Keep the sensor 0.5–1.2 cm above the ground.
3. Wait for completion.
    - Calibration is complete when the 🟢 green calibration indicator turns off.
    - Calibration values are saved automatically. Recalibration is not required at the next startup if environmental conditions remain the same.

## Hardware Indicators and Controls

| Indicator / Control | Description |
| --- | --- |
| 🟢 Green Heartbeat Indicator (near the connector) | Flashes once per second to indicate that the sensor is receiving power and the system is operating normally. |
| 🟢 Green Calibration Indicator (near the calibration button) | After the button is held for 0.5 seconds, this indicator stays on for 5 seconds while calibration is in progress. It turns off when calibration is completed successfully. |
| 🔴 Red Indicator (communication debug indicator) | A steady light indicates a communication failure with the controller. Check that the cable is securely connected, or try another controller port or cable to isolate the issue. |
| 🔘 One-Button Calibration | Hold for 0.5 seconds and release to enter calibration mode. The green calibration indicator stays on for 5 seconds. |
| 💡 Onboard 8-Channel Status LED Array | An illuminated LED indicates that the corresponding channel detects the line. |
