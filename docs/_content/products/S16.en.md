The LineSensor16 is a high-precision, 16-channel line-following sensor designed for WRO robotics competitions. Featuring <span style="color: red;">16-channel infrared detection</span> and an <span style="color: red;">intelligent interference-rejection algorithm</span>, it reduces false detections caused by colored patterns on complex tracks. It supports one-button, 5-second calibration and provides high-resolution line position, line width, and sensor status data. Combined with PID control, it enables smoother, more stable, and more precise line following.

## Before You Begin

1. Confirm that your controller matches the version of your MBC S16 Line-Following Sensor.
2. Connect your MBC S16 Line-Following Sensor to the controller using the appropriate cable.
3. After power is supplied, the green heartbeat LED near the connector will begin flashing.

## Calibration and Normalization

1. Press the calibration button.
    - Press and hold the sensor’s calibration button for 0.5 seconds until the 🟢 green calibration LED near the button stays on.
2. Scan the track for 5 seconds.
    - Move the sensor back and forth across the black line and white areas of the track.
    - Scan the darkest and brightest areas.
    - Keep the sensor 0.5–1.2 cm above the surface.
3. Wait for completion.
    - Calibration is complete when the 🟢 green calibration LED turns off.
    - Calibration values are saved automatically. Recalibration is not required at the next startup if environmental conditions remain unchanged.

## Hardware Indicators and Controls

| Indicator / Control | Description |
| --- | --- |
| 🟢 Green Heartbeat LED (near the connector) | Flashes once per second to indicate that the sensor is powered and operating normally. |
| 🟢 Green Calibration LED (near the calibration button) | After the button is held for 0.5 seconds, this LED stays on for 5 seconds to indicate that calibration is in progress. It turns off when calibration finishes successfully. |
| 🔴 Red LED (communication debug indicator) | A continuously lit LED indicates a communication failure with the controller. Check that the cable is securely connected, or try another controller port or cable to isolate the issue. |
| 🔘 One-Button Calibration Control | Press and hold for 0.5 seconds, then release to enter calibration mode. The green calibration LED stays on for 5 seconds. |
| 💡 Onboard 16-Channel Status LED Array | A lit LED indicates that the corresponding channel detects the black line. |
